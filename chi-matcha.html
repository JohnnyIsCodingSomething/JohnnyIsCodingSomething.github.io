The problem

Ordering a customized drink at a cafe is slow.

The idea: the app lets people pick a branch, browse the menu, and set ice and sugar levels before ordering.

This was the team's starting point. It is not something I measured with user research.

The path through the app

  1. Sign in

    Sign in, or create an account first.

  2. Home

    Choose Our Menu or Our Stores.

  3. Pick a drink

    Five matcha drinks, each with a photo and a price.

  4. Customize

    Set the ice level and the sweet level.

  5. Details

    Add a quantity and optional notes.

  6. Order

    Place the order.

Our Stores is a separate branch page with the address and opening hours.

The screens

What I would improve next

I found these when I reviewed the file again for this page.

  • Prices do not match. The item pages for the other four drinks still show Rp. 45.000, while the menu lists different prices (Matcha Affogato is Rp. 55.000 on the menu).
  • Typos in the copy. The Home screen text has “mmfor” and “yyou”, and the sugar options say “Suger”.
  • Low contrast on the grey labels. Labels such as “Ice Level” use #8C8C8C on the sage background, about 2.8 to 1. The common guideline for small text is 4.5 to 1, so I would darken them.
  • Dropdown positions. The Ice and Sweet dropdowns sit at slightly different positions from one item page to the next.
  • Empty space. The Home and Our Stores screens leave a lot of unused space at the bottom.
  • Real users. I have not tested the ordering flow with real users yet. That would be the first thing I do next.
